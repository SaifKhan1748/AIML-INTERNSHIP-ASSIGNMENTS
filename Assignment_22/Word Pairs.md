## Assignment (27/03/2026)

# Assignment Name : Semantic Meaning
# Description : Find 5 word pairs and explain semantic similarity.

# 1. Car – Automobile
    These two words have almost the same meaning.
👉 Both refer to a vehicle used for transportation.
✔ Highly similar (Synonyms)

# 2. Happy – Joyful
    Both words express a feeling of happiness or pleasure.
👉 The emotional meaning is very close.
✔ Highly similar (Synonyms)

# 3. Teacher – Student
    These words are related but have opposite roles.
👉 A teacher teaches, and a student learns.
✔ Moderately similar (Related concepts)

# 4. Dog – Animal
    A dog is a type of animal.
👉 This shows a category relationship (specific → general).
✔ Moderately similar (Hierarchical relation)

# 5. Hot – Cold
    These words have opposite meanings.
👉 They represent different extremes of temperature.
❌ Low similarity (Antonyms)
